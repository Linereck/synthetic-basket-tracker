The inverted cliff: fails are suppressed to exit, then explode

last 3 days on list first 5 days after exit

Leaving the list requires five consecutive clean days, and the tails of XRT’s streaks show exactly that: fails managed down to a few thousand shares. Then, within five days of exiting, fails jump — up to ×85. Deliveries track the rule’s measurement windows, not economic settlement.

What history says about a residency that has reached day 41

live residency historical median historical IQR

Of fourteen completed residencies, only three ever reached day 41 — one exited the very next day (42), the other two ran 114 and 187 days. That is the entire precedent set: treat it as scenario weights, not probability. The live path shows fails of 100K–380K at the data edge — nothing like the near-zero “exit signature” that historically precedes departures by ~5 days.

Weekly fails for GME, its wrapper (XRT) and its alleged basket peers. CHWY's fails correlate with GME's (+0.38 weekly, log scale) yet it has never appeared on a threshold list — its share count keeps fails below the 0.5% trigger, the same denominator effect that retired GME from the list after 2021. KOSS's only listing ever is a delayed echo in April 2021, three months after squeeze week put ~5% of its float in fails. The list catches small denominators; the fails data catches everything.
